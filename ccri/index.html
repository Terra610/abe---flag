What CCRI Does

CCRI measures how unconstitutional gatekeeping suppresses access to lawful movement, economic participation, and constitutional capital. It does not score people. It scores the divergence of systems, rules, and enforcement dependencies that convert rights into conditional access points.

  • Flags unlawful dependence on DMV or DOT-linked status for non-regulated populations,
  • Measures constitutional divergence created by licensing and enforcement proxies,
  • Estimates the recoverable constitutional capital suppressed by those barriers.

CCRI is a structural measurement module. Its outputs are meant to feed downstream recovery and reinvestment analysis, not consumer profiling.

Inputs

CCRI reads scenarios defined in ccri/inputs.json. Each scenario represents a specific policy environment, institution, or rule set to be tested for constitutional divergence.

Edit ccri/inputs.json in the repo, then refresh this page to recompute results locally in-browser.
