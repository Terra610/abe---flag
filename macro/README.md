# Macro — Aggregate Impact & System Scaling

## Function

Macro aggregates outputs from the A.B.E. system to model total economic impact and large-scale recovery.

It converts system-level divergence and recovery into economy-wide outcomes.

---

## Input

- CII output (unified system result)
- CIBS output (system behavior and compounding effects)
- AFFE output (financial exposure)
- CIRI output (recovery and impact)

---

## Output

- Total economic drag (from divergence)
- Total economic uplift (from realignment)
- Sector-level impact
- Cross-industry scaling effects
- Aggregate system recovery

---

## Calculation Logic

Macro performs:

- Aggregation of all divergence-related costs
- Aggregation of all recovery outputs
- Modeling of compounding system effects
- Projection of cross-domain economic impact

Macro reflects the combined effect of all evaluated conditions.

---

## Deterministic Rule

All divergence contributes to total economic drag.

All recovery contributes to total economic uplift.

System-wide outcomes are the sum of all module outputs.

---

## System Position

Upstream:  
- CII (unified output)  
- CIBS (system behavior)  
- AFFE (financial exposure)  
- CIRI (recovery)

Downstream:  
- Final output layer  
- Strategic modeling and projections

---

## System Role

Macro represents the full-scale impact of the A.B.E. system.

It translates individual divergence conditions into total economic consequences and recovery potential.

---

## Output Dependency

- Final outputs are derived from Macro aggregation
- Strategic planning and large-scale modeling rely on Macro outputs

---

## Core Principle

Systems scale.

Divergence scales into economic drag.  
Realignment scales into economic uplift.

All domains contribute to total system outcomes.

---

## Result

Macro produces a deterministic aggregate model of economic impact.

This output defines the full scale of divergence and recovery across the A.B.E. system.
