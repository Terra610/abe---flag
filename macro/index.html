What MACRO Does

MACRO takes recovery capital from the A.B.E. pipeline and models how that value can ripple through major sectors such as transportation, housing, workforce, community rebuild, small business, health support, and innovation.

Plain language: 

Recovered value
→ sector allocation
→ economic multiplier
→ modeled gain
→ jobs supported
→ broader cascade effect
