Local-only · deterministic · constitutional fidelity engine

A.B.E. — American Butterfly Effect

A constitutional measurement engine that maps authority, measures divergence, quantifies harm, and models recovery through lawful realignment.

Constitutional Engine Spine

A.B.E. does not treat law as abstract commentary. It treats the Constitution as the top tier of governing authority and measures whether downstream conduct, funding, and enforcement remain aligned with it. The engine follows a deterministic firing order: 

Intake → CDA → CDI → CFF → AFFE → CIRI → CIBS → CII → Macro → Integration

The purpose is not interpretation for its own sake. The purpose is to identify divergence from constitutional fidelity, quantify the cost of that divergence, and model the path back to lawful alignment.

What A.B.E. Measures

Authority and Jurisdiction

Whether the claimed action is actually authorized under the constitutional hierarchy of law, whether it is ultra vires, and whether it is void ab initio from inception.

Funding and Mission Scope

Whether public funding, program conditions, and delegated purpose actually authorize the conduct, or whether the action is off-mission and outside approved scope.

Rights and Process

Whether conduct burdens rights, violates due process, ignores lawful limits, or creates measurable societal and economic harm through constitutional divergence.

Recovery and Reinvestment

How lawful realignment converts loss into recoverable value, budget allocation, implementation units, and macro-level public gain.

Core Modules

CDA

Authority, doctrine, jurisdiction, rights burden, ultra vires, off-mission, and void ab initio audit.

CDI

Constitution Divergence Index. Weighted domain-level measurement of deviation from constitutional fidelity.

CFF

Funding fidelity and mission-scope review. Determines whether conduct aligns with approved program authority.

AFFE

Exposure and loss modeling. Converts validated divergence into measurable financial and enforcement exposure.

CIRI

Constitutional Impact and Recovery Index. Translates divergence and exposure into cases avoided, days avoided, and recoverable value.

CIBS

Community Integrity Budget System. Converts recovery into transparent reinvestment and budget allocation.

CII

Constitutional Implementation Interface. Converts budgets into visible implementation units and funded action.

Macro

System-level uplift modeling. Shows how constitutional realignment scales into public prosperity and economic gain.

Integration

Runs the engine chain, checks readiness, shows artifacts, and produces an auditable view of the full system.

Doctrine and System Layers

Doctrine

Constitutional fidelity, void ab initio, and related doctrines define the legal rules that keep the engine anchored to lawful hierarchy instead of drift and interpretation.

System

The system layer exposes integrity, architecture, and audit structure so the engine can be verified, not merely asserted.

Purpose

A.B.E. exists to measure constitutional divergence as real harm, trace that harm through authority, funding, rights, and public cost, and map the route back to constitutional fidelity. The goal is not acceptable drift. The goal is lawful alignment.
