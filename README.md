# 🦋 A.B.E. — American Butterfly Effect (Flag Engine)
**A procedural integrity and alignment framework.**  
Local-first · Source-anchored · Non-narrative · Non-accusatory

---

## What A.B.E. Is

A.B.E. (American Butterfly Effect) is a **constraint-first procedural analysis framework**.

It exists to help people:
- organize records,
- map timelines,
- trace authority,
- and identify where **process aligns or diverges** from governing rules.

A.B.E. does **not** determine guilt, innocence, intent, or motive.  
It does **not** investigate, speculate, or construct narratives.

Its role is structural clarity — nothing more, nothing less.

---

## What A.B.E. Is Not

A.B.E. is **not**:
- legal advice,
- a court,
- a fact-finder,
- an investigative tool,
- a conspiracy engine,
- a predictive system,
- or a storytelling platform.

A.B.E. does not “solve cases.”  
It preserves **process integrity** so qualified humans can review matters responsibly.

---

## Core Principles (Non-Negotiable)

- **Constraint First**  
  Authority, jurisdiction, and procedure are resolved before interpretation.

- **Record Bound**  
  Outputs are anchored to documented materials only.

- **Neutral Language**  
  No accusations, conclusions, or emotional framing.

- **Silence Matters**  
  Absence in the record is labeled — never filled in.

- **Local-First Privacy**  
  No forced uploads, no retention, no tracking.

---

## What the Flag Engine Does

The ABE Flag Engine focuses on **procedural alignment**, not outcomes.

It can:
- classify documents (transcripts, orders, dockets, filings),
- build timelines from documented dates,
- identify where required steps appear or do not appear in the record,
- surface timing-sensitive checkpoints (without conclusions),
- generate **neutral, process-focused questions** for counsel review.

It cannot:
- infer intent,
- connect actors without proof,
- expand scope beyond provided materials,
- or declare wrongdoing.
