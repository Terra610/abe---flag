# A.B.E. — American Butterfly Effect

## Deterministic Constitutional Measurement Engine

A.B.E. is a deterministic system that evaluates conditions against constitutional constraints, measures divergence, and quantifies resulting economic and social impact.

All inputs are processed.  
Conditions either align with constitutional authority or they do not.  
Misalignment is measured as divergence.

---

## Core Function

A.B.E. performs three operations:

1. Constitutional validation  
2. Divergence measurement  
3. Recovery calculation through lawful realignment  

The system does not interpret, predict, persuade, or speculate.

It calculates.

## Modules

Each module performs a defined calculation and extends the system state.

The system is cumulative. No module operates in isolation.

### Intake
Transforms real-world input into structured, traceable data artifacts.

### CDA — Constitutional Divergence Analysis
Determines whether authority exists and whether conditions align with constitutional constraints.  
If not, divergence is established.

### CFF — Constitutional Funding Fidelity
Validates whether funding is lawfully tied to authority and scope.  
Misalignment confirms unauthorized execution.

### AFFE — Applied Fiscal & Financial Exposure
Quantifies the measurable financial cost of divergence.

### CIRI — Constitutional Impact & Recovery Index
Models real-world consequences and recovery through lawful realignment.

### CIBS — Constitutional Impact & Behavior System
Measures how divergence and recovery propagate across interconnected systems.

### CII — Constitutional Integration Interface
Unifies all calculations into a single, traceable system state.

### Macro
Aggregates system-wide results across sectors, domains, industries, and markets.

## Divergence

Divergence occurs when:

- authority is exceeded  
- funding is misapplied  
- jurisdiction is violated  
- scope is exceeded  
- definitions conflict with controlling authority  

Divergence produces:

- economic cost  
- social harm  
- suppressed participation  
- system instability  

All divergence is measurable.

---

## Funding Principle

Funding defines operational authority.

- Actions must align with funded scope  
- Off-scope actions are divergence  
- Misapplied funding is traceable  
- Funding cannot be separated from authority  

---

## Recovery

Removal of divergence produces measurable recovery.

Recovery includes:

- restored mobility  
- increased labor participation  
- housing stabilization  
- reduced incarceration  
- improved economic participation  
- reduced systemic drag  

Recovery propagates through the system.

## Macro Effect

Realignment scales into economic uplift.
