# CIRI — Constitutional Impact & Recovery Index

## Function

CIRI models the real-world impact of divergence and calculates recovery through constitutional realignment.

Divergence produces measurable harm.  
Realignment produces measurable recovery.

---

## Input

- CDA output (divergence classification)
- CFF output (funding misalignment)
- AFFE output (financial exposure)
- Social and economic indicators (housing, employment, incarceration, mobility)

---

## Output

- Recovery potential (economic and social)
- Impact metrics (jobs, housing, mobility, income stabilization)
- Jail days avoided / cases avoided
- Restored participation (workforce, market, community)

---

## Calculation Logic

CIRI calculates:

- Harm caused by divergence (displacement, incarceration, economic suppression)
- Recovery through realignment (restored mobility, employment, housing stability)
- Net gain from eliminating divergence conditions

Recovery is derived from reversing divergence.

---

## Deterministic Rule

Divergence produces harm.

Removal of divergence produces recovery.

Recovery is measurable and directly tied to the elimination of divergence conditions.

---

## System Position

Upstream:  
- CDA (divergence classification)  
- CFF (funding alignment)  
- AFFE (financial exposure)

Downstream:  
- CIBS (system behavior and feedback)  
- Macro (aggregate recovery and economic uplift)

---

## System Role

CIRI translates financial exposure into real-world outcomes.

It quantifies how realignment restores economic participation, stabilizes communities, and reduces systemic harm.

---

## Output Dependency

- CIBS uses CIRI output to evaluate system-wide behavior and response
- Macro aggregates recovery into total economic uplift
- Rebuild Together allocations are derived from CIRI recovery outputs

---

## Core Principle

Divergence suppresses participation.

Realignment restores participation.

All recovery is traceable to the removal of divergence.

---

## Result

CIRI produces a deterministic model of impact and recovery.

This output defines the measurable benefits of constitutional realignment within A.B.E.
