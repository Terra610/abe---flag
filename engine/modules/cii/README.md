# CII — Community Impact & Integrity (Project Portfolios)

**Purpose**:  
Takes the budget allocation from CIBS and converts recovered constitutional capital into concrete, tangible community projects and portfolios. Shows exactly where the money goes and what real-world impact it creates.

**Part of Firing Order**:  
... → cibs → **cii** → macro

**Core Responsibilities**:
- Transforms CIBS allocations into named projects with clear metrics
- Focuses on housing restoration, public safety realignment, youth centers, job training, and soil health
- Ensures transparency: every project is traceable back to constitutional capital (not new taxes)
- Produces auditable project portfolios with SHA-256 receipt

**Inputs**:
- `derived.cibs` (allocation breakdown)
- `derived.ciri` (constitutional capital unlocked)

**Outputs**:
- `derived.cii` — detailed project portfolio with names, amounts, and impact metrics

**Key Features**:
- Fully deterministic and local-only
- Supports original ABE formulas and 2σ–4σ divergence layer
- Transparency-first: clear project-level visibility

It exists to show how ending unconstitutional practices creates real community benefit. No monetization allowed.
