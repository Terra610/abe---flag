# CIBS — Constitutional Impact & Behavior System

## Function

CIBS models how divergence and recovery propagate through interconnected systems.

All systems are linked.  
Divergence compounds across systems.  
Realignment produces compounding recovery.

---

## Input

- CIRI output (impact and recovery)
- AFFE output (financial exposure)
- CDA output (divergence classification)
- Cross-domain indicators (labor, housing, credit, enforcement, health)

---

## Output

- System behavior mapping
- Feedback loops (negative and positive)
- Compounding impact chains
- Stabilization pathways

---

## Calculation Logic

CIBS evaluates:

- How divergence in one domain affects others
- How suppression of participation propagates across systems
- How recovery in one domain accelerates recovery in others

Examples:

- Mobility restriction → reduced employment → reduced income → reduced credit → reduced consumption
- Restored mobility → increased employment → stabilized income → improved credit → increased economic activity

---

## Deterministic Rule

Systems are interconnected.

Divergence compounds across systems.  
Realignment compounds across systems.

No domain operates independently.

---

## System Position

Upstream:  
- CIRI (impact and recovery)  
- AFFE (financial exposure)  
- CDA (divergence classification)

Downstream:  
- CII (integration and synthesis)  
- Macro (aggregate system-wide outcomes)

---

## System Role

CIBS models second-order and cascading effects.

It identifies how divergence and recovery propagate across domains and industries.

---

## Output Dependency

- CII integrates system behavior into a unified output
- Macro aggregates compounding effects into total economic impact

---

## Core Principle

All systems are connected.

Divergence in one domain creates downstream effects in others.

Recovery in one domain produces cascading benefits across systems.

---

## Result

CIBS produces a deterministic model of system behavior and feedback.

This output defines how divergence and recovery scale across the full A.B.E. system.
