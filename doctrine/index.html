Doctrine pages define the rules the system follows. These are not opinions, not interpretations, and not dynamic outputs. They are structural anchors used by the A.B.E. system.

Constitutional Fidelity

Establishes that all enforcement, funding, and regulatory action must trace back to valid, delegated constitutional authority.

Preemption & Supremacy

Federal law controls where conflict exists. State action outside that boundary is void.

Void for Vagueness

Laws lacking clear definition or enforceable boundary fail constitutional standards.

Ultra Vires

Actions taken beyond granted authority are invalid regardless of outcome or intent.

Spending Clause Integrity

Public funds must be tied directly to authorized purpose. Off-mission spending breaks traceability.

Commerce Boundary

Regulation tied to commerce cannot be extended into non-commercial private activity.

Acquiescence

Government power expands when individuals and institutions fail to challenge unauthorized action.

Constitutional Systems Engineering

Defines governance as a structured system where authority, funding, and execution must remain traceable, bounded, and internally coherent.

Due Process Integrity

Legal proceedings must preserve notice, fairness, and the opportunity to be heard without coercion.

Equal Protection

Enforcement and access to law must apply equally without arbitrary classification or selective application.
