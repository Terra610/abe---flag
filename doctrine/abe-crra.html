ABE-CRRA

Citizens Rights Restoration architecture through lawful structural correction

← Doctrine
Core A.B.E. doctrine · static reference · linked PDF

ABE-CRRA is the restoration doctrine inside the A.B.E. engine. It is not limited to identifying structural failure. It defines the recovery path when rights, authority boundaries, and lawful public purpose have already been compromised.

Core Meaning

ABE-CRRA stands for structural restoration, not rhetorical reform. Where authority has drifted, rights have been burdened, or public systems have operated off mission, this doctrine defines how correction becomes measurable recovery.

Restoration Rule

Detect invalid structure
→ identify rights burden
→ isolate causal public harm
→ restore lawful boundary
→ model recovery and redirection

Why It Matters

It is not enough to prove that a system is misaligned. ABE-CRRA exists to move from diagnosis into repair, so structural correction produces lawful recovery rather than abstract critique.

A.B.E. System Role

CDA identifies divergence and invalid conditions.

CFF identifies funding misalignment and off-mission resource use.

CIRI models recovery, savings, and stabilization after correction.

CIBS and CII convert modeled recovery into transparent public-facing outputs.

Operational Logic

Rights deprivation should not remain a dead-end finding.
Structural harm should not remain unquantified.
Restoration must be tied to traceable authority, measurable repair, and lawful redirection.

Recovery Consequence

Under ABE-CRRA, invalid burdens and off-mission public action do not just create legal defects. They create measurable restoration opportunities that can be modeled, budgeted, and tracked.

Linked Modules

Integrity Rule

Restoration must follow lawful correction.
Recovery must remain traceable.
Redirection must remain bounded by public purpose.
The engine should quantify repair, not invent it.

Public Meaning

ABE-CRRA turns “this system is wrong” into “this is how lawful correction restores people, rights, and public value.”
