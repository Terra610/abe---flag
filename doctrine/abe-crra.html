ABE — Citizens’ Rights Restoration Act (CRRA)

Restoration and realignment doctrine for the A.B.E. authority layer.

ABE-CRRA defines the restoration pathway linking constitutional divergence to public recovery, funding integrity, and measurable realignment. In the A.B.E. system, it operates as the primary restoration doctrine that later modules can translate into allocation, propagation, and macro recovery.
