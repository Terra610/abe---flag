Constitutional Fidelity

Authority must remain traceable, bounded, and real

← Doctrine
Core A.B.E. doctrine · static reference · linked PDF

Constitutional Fidelity is the governing anchor of the A.B.E. engine. It requires that every act, enforcement step, funding stream, and claimed authority remain traceable to valid constitutional delegation instead of administrative drift, institutional habit, or circular reasoning.

Core Meaning

Constitutional Fidelity means the system must remain faithful to its authorizing source. Where the source does not authorize the act, the act does not gain legitimacy from repetition, policy preference, or bureaucratic convenience.

Authority Rule

Constitution
→ delegated authority
→ valid scope
→ lawful action

Break the chain and the action loses integrity.

Why It Matters

Without constitutional fidelity, institutions can drift beyond delegated limits while still appearing procedurally normal. That is how invalid power becomes routine.

A.B.E. System Role

Intake captures facts that may implicate authority boundaries.

CDA tests whether claimed authority remains aligned with constitutional delegation.

CFF tests whether funding remains tied to valid mission and authorized purpose.

Integration checks whether the full chain remains traceable to lawful origin.

Operational Logic

If authority is claimed, it must be named.
If authority is named, it must be delegated.
If it is delegated, it must stay within scope.
If it exceeds scope, the engine should flag divergence.

System Consequence

Constitutional Fidelity is what keeps the engine from becoming persuasion theater. It prevents the system from treating administrative assertion as equal to lawful authority.

Linked Modules

Integrity Rule

Claimed power is not enough.
Institutional repetition is not enough.
Procedure alone is not enough.

Authority must remain constitutionally anchored.

Public Meaning

If power cannot show where it came from, what limits it, and why it applies, the system should not pretend the answer is obvious.
